Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026. Applies to the Ritualia Android app.

The short version.

Your routine, your history and your settings are stored on your phone and nowhere else. There is no account to create and no server holding your data. Nothing measures how you use the app — no usage tracking, no advertising identifiers.

One thing does reach the internet: Ritualia Pro. Each time the app opens it checks with RevenueCat, the service that keeps track of whether a purchase is active, and buying itself goes through Google Play. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated identifier and your purchase status — never what is in your routine. This check runs whether or not you ever buy anything.

How to delete your data →

Deleting your data

Ritualia keeps your routine on your phone, so deleting it is something you do on the device rather than something you ask us to do. There is no account to close.

  1. If you subscribe to Ritualia Pro, cancel it first. In the Google Play Store, open Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, choose Ritualia and cancel. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription, and cancelling does not delete your data — the two are separate.
  2. Erase your routine. In Ritualia, open Settings → Erase all data and confirm with Erase everything. Before you confirm, the app tells you how many products, linked pairs and logged days are about to go. It cannot be undone.
  3. To remove everything, uninstall the app. That takes the database, your preferences, and the anonymous identifier RevenueCat stored on the device.

What “Erase all data” removes: your products and the rules attached to them, linked pairs, every day you ticked off or skipped, your pauses, and your routines.

What it keeps: your preferences — display name, avatar, theme, reminder times, and the hour at which your day rolls over. Reminders you switched on stay on and keep arriving at their usual times, on an empty routine, until you turn them off or uninstall. Your Ritualia Pro purchase is also unaffected, so starting over does not cost you it.

Backups you exported are copies in your own hands. Erasing or uninstalling does not reach them — delete those wherever you sent them.

The purchase record. If you would like the record RevenueCat holds — the anonymous identifier and your purchase status — deleted, write to ritualia.support@gmail.com and say so. Requests are acted on within 30 days. Google Play keeps its own record of the transaction under Google’s policy; that one is not ours to delete.

How long anything is kept. Data on your phone stays there until you erase it or uninstall the app. We hold no copy of it, so there is no retention period on our side and nothing of yours waiting to expire. The purchase record at RevenueCat is kept for as long as Ritualia Pro is offered, or until you ask for it to be deleted.

What is stored on your device

Everything you enter into Ritualia is written to a database on the device itself: your products and the rules attached to them, the steps you tick off, skipped days and pauses, your routines and categories, your display name and avatar, your theme, and your reminder times. This data is not transmitted to us, because there is no account system and no server to transmit it to.

Uninstalling the app removes this database, and with it your history. Ritualia offers an export so you can keep a copy before you do.

What leaves your device

Only purchase-related information. Ritualia asks whether your Pro purchase is active each time the app opens, so this happens whether or not you ever buy anything.

  • Google Play processes the payment. Ritualia never sees or handles your payment details; that transaction is between you and Google under Google’s own terms and privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat records whether a purchase is active, so that Pro keeps working after a reinstall or on a second device. Ritualia does not create an account for you and does not send RevenueCat your name or email address. Instead, the RevenueCat software generates a random anonymous identifier, stores it on your device, and uses it to associate the purchase. It is created the first time the app opens, and the app asks about your purchase at every launch after that. Alongside it, RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription status and standard technical information about the app and device, such as the app version, the platform and the store country. RevenueCat also turns these purchase records into sales and subscription reporting for Ritualia — revenue figures, never anything about how you use the app.

Reminders

The optional morning and evening reminders are scheduled and delivered by your device. They are off until you switch them on, they are not sent from a server, and turning them on does not create a push notification token or register you anywhere.

Backups you create yourself

The export feature produces a file containing your rules, history and settings, and hands it to your device’s share sheet. Where that file then goes — a messaging app, a cloud drive, your own storage — is your choice, and once it leaves Ritualia it is governed by whatever service you sent it to. Importing a backup replaces the data currently in the app.

What Ritualia does not do

  • No usage tracking and no advertising identifiers.
  • No advertising, and no sale or sharing of personal data with advertisers.
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no password, no email collection inside the app.
  • No access to your photos, contacts, location or health records.

Children

Ritualia is not directed at children and is not designed for use by them. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

Because your routine data lives only on your device, you control it directly. The steps for erasing it, and exactly what each one removes, are under Deleting your data above. To manage or cancel a Ritualia Pro subscription, use your Google Play account’s subscription settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be described in the app’s release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data: ritualia.support@gmail.com.
