2. What the app is

Ritualia is a personal routine tracker. You enter products and the rules for how often each one repeats, and the app works out which steps are due on a given day and records what you completed.

Ritualia is a routine tracker, not a medical device. It does not dose, diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and it does not verify that a schedule you enter is appropriate for you.
